Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-25

This Privacy Policy explains how Doan Quoc Hieu ("we", "us", "our") collects, uses, stores and deletes information when you use the Monevo mobile application and API (the "Service").

1. Who we are

Monevo is operated by Doan Quoc Hieu, an individual developer based in Vietnam, and is distributed on Google Play under that developer account. For privacy questions or requests, contact us at doanquochieu2104@gmail.com.

2. Information we collect

Account information

  • Email address and display name.
  • If you choose Google Sign-In: the Google account email, name and profile photo that Google shares with us through Clerk.
  • Authentication identifiers issued by our identity provider (Clerk) and an internal user identifier used to link your records.
  • If you set a password, it is stored only as a salted hash. We never store your password in readable form.

Financial information you enter

  • Wallet names, types and balances.
  • Income and expense transactions, notes and dates.
  • Categories, monthly budgets and bill reminders.

This information is stored so the Service can show it back to you. We do not connect to your bank, we never receive your bank credentials or card numbers, and we do not move money.

Usage and analytics data

We use PostHog, a product analytics provider, to understand how the app is used and to fix what is confusing. PostHog receives:

  • App events such as screens opened and buttons tapped. The app automatically captures taps to record these events.
  • A random installation identifier, app version, device model, operating system version, language and an approximate region derived from your IP address.
  • Your internal user identifier while you are signed in, so we can tell a returning user from a new one. We do not send your email address, your name, your transaction amounts, your notes or your budgets to PostHog.

Diagnostics and error data

When the app or API fails, Sentry, our error monitoring provider, receives the error message, a stack trace and technical request details such as the route, status code, timing and app version. We do not intentionally include your financial records in these reports.

Notifications

Bill reminders are scheduled locally on your device and their content is not sent to our servers. We only learn whether you granted the notification permission.

We do not collect precise location, contacts, photos, microphone, health data or advertising identifiers. We do not use advertising SDKs and we do not sell personal data.

3. How we use information

  • To create and secure your account and sign you in.
  • To store and display the financial records you enter.
  • To schedule bill reminder notifications you enable.
  • To understand which features are used and improve the Service.
  • To detect, diagnose and fix errors and abuse.
  • To respond to support and privacy requests.

4. Legal bases

Where applicable, we process information to perform our contract with you, to comply with legal obligations, to protect the Service, and based on your consent (for example, notification permission, which you can revoke in your device settings, and analytics, which you can opt out of as described below).

5. Sharing and service providers

We share information only with providers that help us run the Service:

  • Clerk — authentication and session management.
  • Google — only if you choose Google Sign-In.
  • Amazon Web Services — hosting for the API and PostgreSQL database in the ap-southeast-2 (Sydney) region.
  • Expo — application build and distribution tooling.
  • PostHog — product analytics, processing usage events described above.
  • Sentry (Functional Software, Inc.) — error monitoring and diagnostics.

These providers process data on our behalf and are not permitted to use it for their own advertising. We may also disclose information when required by law or to protect rights and safety.

6. Storage, security and retention

Account and financial records are stored in our PostgreSQL database on AWS. Sign-in credentials are handled by Clerk over encrypted HTTPS connections, passwords are stored only as salted hashes, server access is restricted to authorized operators, and authentication tokens on your device are kept in the platform secure store. Our legal pages are served over HTTPS.

We retain your information while your account exists. When you delete your account, we delete your account and associated financial records. Residual copies may remain in encrypted backups for a limited period (normally no more than 30 days) before being overwritten. Analytics events are retained for up to 12 months, error diagnostics for up to 90 days, and records we must keep by law only for as long as the law requires.

7. Your rights and choices

Depending on your location, you may have the right to:

  • Access and receive a copy of your information.
  • Correct inaccurate account information.
  • Delete your account and associated data.
  • Withdraw consent (for example, disable notifications).
  • Opt out of product analytics.
  • Object to or restrict certain processing.
  • Complain to your local data protection authority.

You can delete your account directly in the app: Profile → Delete Account. You can also use our account deletion page or email doanquochieu2104@gmail.com to exercise any of these rights, including opting out of analytics. We respond to verified requests within 30 days.

8. Children

The Service is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

9. International transfers

Our providers may process information in countries other than yours, including the United States and Australia. Where required, we rely on appropriate safeguards for those transfers.

10. Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy. If we make material changes, we will update the date above and, where appropriate, notify you in the app.

11. Contact
