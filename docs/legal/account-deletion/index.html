Delete your account

You can delete your Monevo account and the data linked to it at any time, without contacting support first.

Last updated 25 October 2026 Applies to the Monevo app and API

In the app

Profile → Delete Account.

By email

doanquochieu2104@gmail.com, subject "Account deletion request".

Effect

Account and financial records are deleted immediately.

Backups

Encrypted copies are overwritten within 30 days.

#Delete in the app (recommended)

You can permanently delete your Monevo account and the data linked to it at any time. There is no charge and you do not need to contact support first.

  1. Open Monevo and sign in.
  2. Go to the Profile tab.
  3. Scroll to the Account section and tap Delete Account.
  4. Confirm the deletion.

You are signed out and your account is removed immediately. Wallets, transactions, categories, budgets and reminders are deleted with it through cascading deletes.

#Delete without the app

If you cannot sign in, email doanquochieu2104@gmail.com from the email address registered to your account, with the subject "Account deletion request". We will verify ownership and complete the deletion within 30 days.

The same address can be used to ask what data is associated with your account or to request deletion of data you believe is still linked to you.

#What is deleted

  • Your account profile: email address, display name, profile photo and authentication identifiers.
  • All wallets and balances you created.
  • All transactions and notes.
  • All categories, budgets and bill reminders.
  • Any active session for the account.

Deletion is permanent. Deleted financial records cannot be recovered, so export anything you need before you confirm.

#What may be retained

Encrypted backup copies of the database may persist for up to 30 days before being overwritten. Product analytics events are linked to a random identifier rather than your identity and are retained for up to 12 months; after your account is deleted they can no longer be connected to you. Error diagnostics are retained for up to 90 days. We may also retain minimal records if required by law, for example for fraud prevention or tax obligations.

#If you signed in with Google

Deleting your Monevo account removes the profile and records we hold. It does not delete your Google account. To remove Monevo's access to your Google account, visit Google Account permissions and remove access for Monevo.
