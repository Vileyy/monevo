# Expo & Frontend Design Rules

1. **Expo Docs**: Read the exact versioned docs at https://docs.expo.dev/versions/v57.0.0/ before writing any code.
2. **Frontend Design Skill (Mandatory)**: Before designing, creating, or modifying any UI/UX or styling, you **MUST** read and strictly follow the [frontend-design skill](file:///.agents/skills/frontend-design/SKILL.md). Ensure deliberate choices for palette, typography, visual hierarchy, and distinct visual identity without falling back to cliché or templated defaults.
