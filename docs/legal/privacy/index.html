Privacy Policy

This policy explains what Monevo collects, why we need it, who processes it, and how you can delete it — in plain language.

Last updated 25 October 2026 Applies to the Monevo app and API

What we collect

Account details and the financial records you enter.

What we never collect

Bank credentials, card numbers, contacts, photos or advertising IDs.

Who processes it

Clerk, Google, AWS, Expo, PostHog and Sentry.

How to delete

In the app via Profile → Delete Account, or by email.

#1. Who we are

Monevo is a personal finance app operated by Doan Quoc Hieu, an individual developer based in Vietnam. For the purposes of data protection law, Doan Quoc Hieu is the data controller of the information described in this policy.

This policy applies to the Monevo mobile application and the API that serves it (together, the "Service"). For privacy questions or requests, email doanquochieu2104@gmail.com.

#2. Information we collect

We collect only what is needed to run the Service. You can use much of the app without entering financial details, but an account is required to store your records.

Account information

  • Email address and display name.
  • If you sign in with Google: the Google account email, name and profile photo that Google shares with us through our identity provider, Clerk.
  • Authentication identifiers issued by Clerk and an internal user identifier used to link your records.
  • If you set a password, it is stored only as a salted hash. We never store your password in readable form.

Financial records you enter

  • Wallet names, types and balances.
  • Income and expense transactions, notes and dates.
  • Categories, monthly budgets and bill reminders.

This information is stored so the Service can show it back to you. We do not connect to your bank, we never receive your bank credentials or card numbers, and we do not move money.

Usage and analytics data

We use PostHog to understand how the app is used and to fix what is confusing. PostHog receives:

  • App events such as screens opened and actions you take. The analytics SDK records these automatically.
  • Technical details attached to each event, such as app version, device model, operating system version and language.
  • An approximate region derived from your IP address.
  • Your internal user identifier while you are signed in, so we can tell a returning user from a new one.

We do not send your email address, your name, your transaction amounts, your notes or your budgets to PostHog.

Diagnostics and error data

When the app or API fails, Sentry receives the error message, a stack trace and technical request details such as the route, status code, timing and app version. We do not intentionally include your financial records in these reports.

Notifications

Bill reminders are scheduled locally on your device and their content is not sent to our servers. We only learn whether you granted the notification permission.

We do not collect precise location, contacts, photos, microphone, health data or advertising identifiers. We do not use advertising SDKs and we do not sell personal data.

#3. How we use information

  • To create and secure your account and sign you in.
  • To store and display the financial records you enter.
  • To schedule bill reminder notifications you enable.
  • To understand which features are used and improve the Service.
  • To detect, diagnose and fix errors and abuse.
  • To respond to support and privacy requests.

We do not use your information for advertising, profiling with legal effects, or automated decisions that affect your rights.

#4. Legal bases

Where data protection law requires a legal basis, we rely on:

  • Performance of a contract — to provide the Service you signed up for.
  • Consent — for notifications and analytics, which you can withdraw at any time.
  • Legitimate interests — to keep the Service secure, prevent abuse and fix errors.
  • Legal obligation — where we must retain or disclose information by law.

#5. Sharing and service providers

We share information only with providers that help us run the Service. They process it on our behalf and are not permitted to use it for their own advertising.

Provider Purpose Processing location
Clerk Authentication and session management. United States
Google Sign-in with Google, only if you choose it. United States
Amazon Web Services (AWS) Hosting for the API and PostgreSQL database. Australia (ap-southeast-2)
Expo Application build and distribution tooling. United States
PostHog Product analytics for screens and actions. United States
Sentry Error monitoring and diagnostics. United States

Google user data

If you sign in with Google, we use your basic profile information (name, email address and profile photo) only to create and display your account. Our use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We do not use Google user data for advertising, and we do not transfer it to third parties except as needed to provide the Service, to comply with law, or with your consent.

We may also disclose information when required by law, to respond to lawful requests, or to protect the rights, property or safety of our users and the Service.

#6. Storage, security and retention

Account and financial records are stored in our PostgreSQL database on AWS. Sign-in credentials are handled by Clerk over encrypted HTTPS connections, passwords are stored only as salted hashes, server access is restricted to authorized operators, and authentication tokens on your device are kept in the platform secure store. Our public pages, including this one, are served over HTTPS.

Data Retention
Account and financial records While your account exists. Deleted when you delete your account.
Encrypted database backups Overwritten within 30 days.
Product analytics events Up to 12 months.
Error diagnostics Up to 90 days.
Records required by law Only for as long as the law requires.

No method of transmission or storage is completely secure. We work to protect your information, but we cannot guarantee absolute security.

#7. Your rights and choices

Depending on your location, you may have the right to:

  • Access and receive a copy of your information.
  • Correct inaccurate account information.
  • Delete your account and associated data.
  • Withdraw consent, for example by disabling notifications.
  • Opt out of product analytics.
  • Object to or restrict certain processing.
  • Complain to your local data protection authority.

You can delete your account directly in the app: Profile → Delete Account. You can also use our account deletion page or email doanquochieu2104@gmail.com to exercise any of these rights, including requesting a copy of your data or opting out of analytics. We respond to verified requests within 30 days.

#8. Children

The Service is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

#9. International transfers

Our providers may process information in countries other than yours, including the United States and Australia. Where required, we rely on appropriate safeguards for those transfers, such as standard contractual clauses.

#10. Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy. If we make material changes, we will update the date at the top of this page and, where appropriate, notify you in the app before the change takes effect.
