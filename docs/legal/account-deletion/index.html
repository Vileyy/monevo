Delete your account

Last updated: 2026-10-25

You can permanently delete your Monevo account and the data linked to it at any time. There is no charge and you do not need to contact support first.

Delete in the app (recommended)

  1. Open Monevo and sign in.
  2. Go to the Profile tab.
  3. Scroll to the Account section and tap Delete Account.
  4. Confirm the deletion.

You will be signed out and your account row is removed immediately. Wallets, transactions, categories, budgets and reminders are deleted with it through cascading deletes.

Delete without the app

If you cannot sign in, email doanquochieu2104@gmail.com from the email address registered to your account, with the subject "Account deletion request". We will verify ownership and complete the deletion within 30 days. This is also the place to request deletion of data you believe is still associated with you.

What is deleted

  • Your account profile: email address, display name, auth identifiers.
  • All wallets and balances you created.
  • All transactions and notes.
  • All categories, budgets and bill reminders.
  • Any active session for the account.

What may be retained

Encrypted backup copies of the database may persist for up to 30 days before being overwritten. Product analytics events are linked to a random identifier rather than your identity and are retained for up to 12 months; after your account is deleted they can no longer be connected to you. Error diagnostics are retained for up to 90 days. We may also retain minimal records if required by law (for example, fraud-prevention or tax obligations).

Questions

Contact Doan Quoc Hieu at doanquochieu2104@gmail.com if you need help with deletion or want to know what data is associated with your account.
